# What one day of pageviews shows

Generated by `scripts/day_findings.py` from the loaded Parquet: 1 day(s), 2024-01-01, 143,351,375 rows, 558,808,250 views. Machine and versions are in the README.

**One day is not representative.** 2024-01-01 is a holiday. Treat every figure as a question for more days, not a conclusion.

- **Mobile dominates:** 77.8% of views come from mobile wikis (codes ending in `.m`). I have not checked how Wikimedia filters automated traffic in these files.
- **A daily rhythm:** the quietest hour (UTC 08:00) has 16,583,353 views and the busiest (21:00) has 30,328,801, a ratio of 1.8x.
- **Biggest wikis by views:** `en.m` 213,800,218, `en` 65,373,407, `de.m` 25,264,031, `ru.m` 23,943,736, `ja.m` 23,369,364, `es.m` 21,256,031.
- **Long tail:** 59.2% of rows have exactly 1 view and carry 15.2% of all views. The 1,000 most-viewed English titles hold 13.5% of English views.
- **Top English pages (excluding Main_Page and meta pages):** New_Year's_Day (1,282,781), New_Year (772,138), Lynyrd_Skynyrd (416,609), Murder_of_Dee_Dee_Blanchard (339,898), Elle_King (336,666).
- **Detectable spikes** (an English page's busiest hour against its median hour, pages with at least 2,000 views in 20 or more hours): Auld_Lang_Syne (21,286 vs 4,412, 4.8x), New_Year's_Eve (16,915 vs 6,189, 2.7x), Frederik,_Crown_Prince_of_Denmark (12,830 vs 5,744, 2.2x).
- **A dead column:** the size field is non-zero in 0 rows, so it carries no information.
- **Scale:** 38,295,525 distinct (wiki, page) pairs in the loaded data.
